# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Climate Learning Framework community is dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone. We are committed to fostering an environment where all participants can:

- Feel safe and respected
- Share ideas and perspectives openly
- Contribute meaningfully to climate action
- Learn and grow together

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All community interactions (GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests)
- Project spaces and events
- Private communications related to the project
- Representation of the project in public spaces

## Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

### 1. **Be Respectful**
- Treat all individuals with dignity and respect
- Listen actively to different viewpoints
- Engage constructively in disagreements
- Use inclusive language

### 2. **Be Collaborative**
- Work constructively with others
- Give and receive feedback gracefully
- Support fellow community members
- Help newer members get involved

### 3. **Be Professional**
- Maintain professional communication
- Focus on the content, not personal attacks
- Acknowledge differing expertise levels
- Value diversity of background and experience

### 4. **Be Honest**
- Acknowledge limitations and gaps in knowledge
- Correct errors promptly
- Cite sources properly
- Avoid spreading misinformation

### 5. **Be Inclusive**
- Welcome newcomers and beginners
- Accommodate diverse communication styles
- Consider global perspectives
- Use accessible language

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are **not tolerated**:

- **Harassment**: Offensive comments, threats, intimidation, or unwelcome contact
- **Discrimination**: Based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or other protected characteristics
- **Hate Speech**: Language that attacks or demeans groups
- **Sexual Harassment**: Unwelcome sexual advances, comments, or imagery
- **Misinformation**: Deliberate spreading of false climate information
- **Spam**: Irrelevant self-promotion or repetitive messages
- **Doxxing/Privacy Violations**: Sharing private information without consent
- **Abuse of Power**: Using moderator/maintainer status unfairly
- **Trolling**: Deliberately provocative behavior intended to disrupt

## Reporting & Enforcement

### How to Report

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document**: Note the behavior, date, time, and context
2. **Report**: Email `climate-framework@example.com` with:
   - Description of the incident
   - Links to relevant content (GitHub URLs, messages, etc.)
   - Any witnesses
   - How it affected you
   - What outcome you'd like to see

3. **Confidentiality**: Reports are handled confidentially
4. **No Retaliation**: We prohibit retaliation against reporters

### Investigation & Response

- **Acknowledgment**: You'll receive acknowledgment within 48 hours
- **Investigation**: We'll investigate thoroughly and fairly
- **Resolution**: Actions may include:
  - Private warnings or education
  - Public apologies
  - Temporary restrictions
  - Permanent removal from community
- **Timeline**: Major violations typically resolved within 2 weeks

## Community Responsibilities

### All Members
- Model expected behavior
- Report violations confidentially
- Support safe community
- Educate others about this code

### Maintainers & Moderators
- Uphold this code consistently
- Address violations promptly
- Provide support to affected members
- Make decisions transparently
- Recuse themselves from decisions if biased

## Diversity & Inclusion

The Climate Learning Framework celebrates:
- Diverse professional backgrounds
- Multiple perspectives on climate solutions
- Different experience levels
- Global and local knowledge
- Various communication styles

We actively work to:
- Ensure equitable participation
- Provide accessible resources
- Welcome underrepresented groups in climate action
- Address systemic barriers

## Conflict Resolution

### For Minor Disagreements
1. **Direct Communication**: Try discussing respectfully with involved parties
2. **Mediation**: Ask a community member to help facilitate
3. **Cool-Off Period**: Step back if emotions run high

### For Serious Issues
- Contact moderators or maintainers
- Follow the formal reporting process
- Participate in restorative discussions if appropriate
